About ASTRA-HEALTH
A student-built astronaut health-management prototype inspired by crew wellbeing during space missions.
Project overview
ASTRA-HEALTH brings example vital signs, wellness tracking, exercise logging, radiation awareness, bone-mass modeling, recreation games, satellite lookup, and mission notes into one responsive interface.
